## Notation

Each symbol has one meaning throughout. Where an exam paper uses a different symbol, the table says so.

| Symbol | Meaning | Section |
|:--|:--|:--|
| $x$, $y$ | quantities of two goods; in Part III, consumer goods and capital goods | {sec:s1}, {sec:s15} |
| $p_{x}$, $p_{y}$, $B$ | prices of the two goods, and the household's budget | {sec:s1} |
| $U$, $MU_{x}$ | utility, and the marginal utility of $x$ | {sec:s1} |
| $\partial$ | a derivative taken with every other variable held fixed | {sec:s1} |
| $D(t)$, $\delta$ | value today of £1 in $t$ periods; discount factor, the value today of £1 next period | {sec:sbeh}, {sec:s12b} |
| $Q$, $K$, $L$, $K_{0}$ | output, capital, labour, and capital held fixed in the short run | {sec:s2} |
| $MP_{L}$, $AP_{L}$ | marginal and average product of labour | {sec:s2} |
| $w$, $R$ | the money wage, and the rental price of capital | {sec:s3} |
| $TC$, $TFC$, $TVC$, $MC$, $AFC$, $AVC$, $ATC$, $LRAC$ | total, fixed, variable, marginal and average costs | {sec:s3}, {sec:s4} |
| $P$, $a$, $b$ | price; intercept and slope of a single firm's inverse demand curve | {sec:s5} |
| $TR$, $AR$, $MR$ | total, average and marginal revenue | {sec:s5} |
| $PED$, $YED$, $XED$, $PES$ | price, income and cross elasticities of demand, and price elasticity of supply | {sec:s1}, {sec:s10} |
| $\Pi$ | profit | {sec:s6} |
| $v(x)$ | value in pounds of consuming $x$ units | {sec:s7} |
| $\alpha$, $\beta$, $\gamma$ | market demand: intercept, response to price, response to income | {sec:s7} |
| $\mu$, $\lambda$ | market supply: intercept, response to price | {sec:s8} |
| $Q_{D}$, $Q_{S}$, $P^{*}$, $Q^{*}$ | quantity demanded and supplied; equilibrium (or efficient) price and quantity | {sec:s9} |
| $MPB$, $MPC$, $TS$ | marginal private benefit and cost, and total surplus. $MPC$ always means marginal private cost; the marginal propensity to consume is $c$ | {sec:s11} |
| $P_{m}$, $Q_{m}$, $P_{c}$, $Q_{c}$ | monopoly price and output, and the competitive comparison | {sec:s12} |
| $MRP_{L}$, $MCL$ | marginal revenue product of labour, and marginal cost of labour | {sec:slab} |
| $MSB$, $MSC$, $MEB$, $MEC$, $Q_{mkt}$ | marginal social and external benefit and cost, and the quantity an unregulated market produces | {sec:s13} |
| $t$, $s$ | a specific tax and a subsidy, per unit | {sec:s14} |
| $P_{D}$, $P_{S}$ | with a tax, the price buyers pay and the price sellers keep | {sec:s14} |
| $C$, $I$, $G$, $X$, $M$ | consumption, investment, government spending on goods and services, exports, imports | {sec:s16} |
| $S$, $T$ | saving, and taxes net of transfers and subsidies | {sec:s16} |
| $c$, $\tau$, $m$ | marginal propensity to consume, tax rate, marginal propensity to import. Exam papers write MPC, MPT and MPM | {sec:s17} |
| $A$, $k$, $z$ | autonomous spending, the multiplier, the fraction of each pound respent at home | {sec:s17} |
| $Y$, $Y_{FE}$ | income (national income in Part III), and full-employment output | {sec:s7}, {sec:s18} |
| $\pi$, $\pi^{e}$, $u$, $u_{n}$, $\omega$ | inflation, expected inflation, unemployment rate, the NAIRU, and the slope of the Phillips curve | {sec:s20} |
| $M_{S}$, $M_{D}$, $V$ | money supply, money demand, and velocity | {sec:s20}, {sec:s22} |
| $\rho$, $D$ | reserve ratio, and bank deposits | {sec:s21} |
| $i$, $i_{B}$, $r$, $\theta$, $\phi$ | nominal interest rate, Bank Rate, real interest rate, and the responses of money demand to income and to $i$ | {sec:s22} |
| $b$, $g$, $\psi$ | public debt as a share of GDP, growth rate of real GDP, primary surplus as a share of GDP | {sec:s23} |
| $\varepsilon$, $\tau^{*}$ | elasticity of the tax base, and the revenue-maximising tax rate | {sec:s23} |
| $\ell(p)$ | the Lorenz curve: share of income received by the poorest fraction $p$ | {sec:sineq} |
| $g_{Y}$, $g_{A}$, $g_{K}$, $g_{L}$ | growth rates of output, productivity, capital and labour | {sec:s24} |
| $e$, $P_{X}$, $P_{M}^{*}$, $TB$ | exchange rate in dollars per pound, sterling price of exports, dollar price of imports, trade balance | {sec:s25} |
| $P_{w}$ | world price | {sec:s27} |
